# Shatomin FAQ v1.1.0

## Service

### Q: If the server's foot touches the line during service, is it a fault?
A: Yes, generally it is a fault. The server and receiver should stand inside the correct service courts and should not touch the boundary lines during service.

Japanese:
サーブのときにサーバーまたはレシーバーの足がサービスコートのラインに触れている場合、一般的にはフォルトです。

Official match note:
In official matches, follow the umpire, service judge, referee, and tournament regulations.
