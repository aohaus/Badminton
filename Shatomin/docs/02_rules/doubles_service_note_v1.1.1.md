# Doubles Service Note v1.1.1

## Purpose

This note helps Shatomin answer common questions about service order in badminton doubles.

Use this note for questions about:

- who serves next in doubles
- right or left service court
- even and odd scores
- service side score
- server and receiver positions
- what happens after winning or losing a rally
- simple explanations for children, parents, and coaches

## Core Rule Summary

In doubles, the serving side's score decides the service court.

- If the serving side's score is even, the serve is made from the right service court.
- If the serving side's score is odd, the serve is made from the left service court.
- The side that wins a rally scores one point.
- The side that wins a rally serves next.
- If the serving side wins the rally, the same server serves again from the other service court.
- If the receiving side wins the rally, the receiving side gets one point and becomes the new serving side.
- Players do not change their service court unless their side wins a point while serving.

- サーブ側の点数が偶数なら、右のサービスコートからサーブ。
- サーブ側の点数が奇数なら、左のサービスコートからサーブ。
- ラリーに勝った側が1点を取る。
- ラリーに勝った側が次にサーブする。
- サーブ側が続けて点を取った場合、同じサーバーが左右を入れ替えてもう一度サーブする。
- レシーブ側が点を取った場合、その側が新しいサーブ側になる。
- サーブ側が点を取った時だけ、そのペアは左右を入れ替える。

## Common Questions and Answers

### Q1. In doubles, how do I know whether to serve from the right or left?

Japanese:
サーブ側の点数を見ます。サーブ側の点数が偶数なら右、奇数なら左からサーブします。

English:
Look at the serving side's score. If it is even, serve from the right. If it is odd, serve from the left.

Short Shatomin style:
点数を見るミン。偶数は右、奇数は左だミン。

### Q2. Who serves next after a rally?

Japanese:
ラリーに勝った側が1点を取り、次にサーブします。

English:
The side that wins the rally gets one point and serves next.

Short Shatomin style:
勝った側が1点を取って、次にサーブするミン。

### Q3. If the serving side wins the rally, who serves next?

Japanese:
同じサーバーが、左右を入れ替えてもう一度サーブします。

English:
The same server serves again, but from the other service court.

Short Shatomin style:
同じ人が、反対側からもう一回サーブするミン。

### Q4. If the receiving side wins the rally, who serves next?

Japanese:
レシーブ側が1点を取り、新しいサーブ側になります。次のサーバーは、その側の点数が偶数か奇数かで決まります。

English:
The receiving side gets one point and becomes the new serving side. The next server is decided by whether that side's score is even or odd.

Short Shatomin style:
レシーブ側が勝ったら、その側が次にサーブするミン。

### Q5. Do both players change sides after every rally?

Japanese:
いいえ。サーブ側が点を取った時だけ、そのペアは左右を入れ替えます。レシーブ側は、そのラリーでは左右を入れ替えません。

English:
No. Only the serving side changes left and right when they win a point while serving. The receiving side does not change sides in that rally.

Short Shatomin style:
毎回みんなが動くわけではないミン。

### Q6. What should we do if we lose track of the server?

Japanese:
まず、現在のサーブ側の点数を確認します。偶数なら右、奇数なら左です。その後、直前にどちらが点を取ったかを思い出します。

English:
First, check the current serving side's score. Even means right, odd means left. Then remember which side won the last rally.

Short Shatomin style:
迷ったら、点数と前のラリーを見るミン。

### Q7. Can the wrong player serve in doubles?

Japanese:
はい。間違った人がサーブした場合、公式戦では主審が判断します。練習では、できるだけ早く気づいて正しい順番に戻しましょう。

English:
Yes. The wrong player can serve by mistake. In official matches, the umpire decides what happens. In practice, correct the order as soon as you notice.

Short Shatomin style:
間違えたら、早めに直すミン。

## Simple Doubles Service Formula

Japanese:

1. サーブ側を見る。
2. サーブ側の点数を見る。
3. 偶数なら右。
4. 奇数なら左。
5. サーブ側が点を取ったら、同じ人が左右を入れ替えてサーブ。
6. レシーブ側が点を取ったら、その側が新しいサーブ側。

English:

1. Look at the serving side.
2. Look at the serving side's score.
3. Even score means right.
4. Odd score means left.
5. If the serving side wins the point, the same player serves again from the other side.
6. If the receiving side wins the point, that side becomes the new serving side.

## Kids Mode Explanation

Japanese:

ダブルスのサーブで迷ったら、こう覚えよう。

- 偶数は右。
- 奇数は左。
- 勝ったチームが次にサーブ。
- サーブ側が続けて勝ったら、同じ人が反対側からサーブ。

English:

If you are confused in doubles, remember this:

- Even score = right.
- Odd score = left.
- The team that wins the rally serves next.
- If the serving team wins again, the same player serves from the other side.

## Coach Mode Tip

Japanese:

ジュニア選手には、まず「サーブ側の点数を見る」を徹底させると分かりやすいです。
最初から複雑なローテーション表を覚えさせるより、偶数右・奇数左を体で覚える練習が効果的です。

English:

For junior players, first teach them to check the serving side's score.
Instead of starting with a complicated rotation chart, help them practice "even-right, odd-left" until it feels natural.

## Parent Mode Explanation

Japanese:

子どもにはこう伝えると分かりやすいです。

「ダブルスのサーブで迷ったら、サーブするチームの点数を見よう。偶数なら右、奇数なら左だよ。」

English:

A simple way to explain it to a child:

"If you are not sure where to serve from in doubles, look at the serving team's score. Even means right, and odd means left."

## Common Mistakes

Japanese:

よくある間違いです。

- 自分たちの点数ではなく、相手の点数を見てしまう
- 「サーブ側の点数」を見ることを忘れる
- レシーブ側も毎回左右を入れ替えると思ってしまう
- サーブ側が続けて点を取った時に、サーバーを変えてしまう
- 偶数右・奇数左を逆に覚える

English:

Common mistakes:

- Looking at the opponent's score instead of the serving side's score
- Forgetting to check the serving side's score
- Thinking that the receiving side changes sides every rally
- Changing the server when the serving side wins another point
- Mixing up even-right and odd-left
